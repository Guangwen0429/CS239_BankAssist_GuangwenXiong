P1. (Lifecycle: authentication first; applies to ConciergeAgent, AuthAgent and AccountAgent) The ConciergeAgent must not send any request to the AccountAgent or the RiskAgent on behalf of a customer until the AuthAgent has replied `verified` for that customer ID in the current conversation and has issued a session token. Every request the AccountAgent receives must carry a session token that `resolve_session` reports as valid (issued by the AuthAgent, not older than 15 minutes); otherwise the AccountAgent must refuse the request without calling any other tool. A failed login must not be retried automatically with the same PIN, by the agents or by the client application; only a new PIN entered by the customer counts. When a session expires during a conversation, the ConciergeAgent may log the customer in again once with the credentials of the current turn.

P2. (Information flow: credentials) A customer's PIN may flow only from the customer to the ConciergeAgent and from the ConciergeAgent to the AuthAgent inside an `authenticate` message, where it may be used only as the `pin` argument of `verify_identity`. The PIN must never be included in a message to the AccountAgent or the RiskAgent, in the argument of any other tool, in any reply to the customer, in the audit log, or in a saved trace (traces must show it as `****`). If the customer types the PIN into a chat message, the ConciergeAgent must mask it before the message is recorded or parsed.

P3. (Lifecycle: lockout; applies to AuthAgent and ConciergeAgent) After 3 consecutive failed PIN attempts for a customer ID, the profile is locked. While it is locked the AuthAgent must refuse to verify that customer even when the correct PIN is given, and must not issue a session token. A successful login resets the failure counter. Unlocking is done by bank staff outside the application; no agent may unlock a profile. When refusing, the ConciergeAgent may tell the customer how many attempts remain or that the profile is locked, but must not reveal whether the PIN was close or which digits were wrong.

P4. (Capability: tool ownership) Each agent may call only its own tools. AuthAgent: `verify_identity`, `issue_session`. AccountAgent: `resolve_session`, `list_accounts`, `get_balance`, `get_transactions`, `transfer_funds`. RiskAgent: `check_payee`, `daily_transfer_total`, `approve_transfer`. ConciergeAgent: `parse_request` only. Every agent may call `record_audit`. An agent that needs something outside its own tools must send a message to the agent that owns the tool; it must never call that tool directly.

P5. (Permission: account ownership; applies to AccountAgent) The AccountAgent may read the balance or transaction history of an account, list accounts, or use an account as the source (`from_account`) of a transfer only if that account is owned by the customer bound to the session token in the request. This holds even when the customer or the ConciergeAgent names the account ID explicitly, and even when the RiskAgent has approved the transfer. Exception: the destination (`to_account`) of a transfer may be any existing account, including another customer's or an external one.

P6. (Information isolation between customers; applies to AccountAgent and ConciergeAgent) The AccountAgent must not return data belonging to one customer (name, account balances, transaction history, saved payees, transfer history) in a reply to a request made under a different customer's session, and the ConciergeAgent must not include such data in a reply to the customer. When a transfer goes to another customer's account, replies to the sender may show only the destination account ID, never the recipient's name, balance or transactions.

P7. (Permission: money movement) Only the AccountAgent may move money, and only by calling `transfer_funds` in response to an `execute_transfer` request from the ConciergeAgent. The RiskAgent may approve but never execute a transfer; the ConciergeAgent and AuthAgent may never execute one.

P8. (Lifecycle: risk approval before execution; applies to ConciergeAgent, RiskAgent and AccountAgent) Every transfer must be assessed by the RiskAgent (`assess_transfer`) before it is executed, and the RiskAgent issues an approval only after its own checks (P9, P10) have passed. The AccountAgent may call `transfer_funds` only with an `approval_id` issued by the RiskAgent's `approve_transfer` for exactly the same `from_account`, `to_account` and `amount`, that has not been used before and is at most 10 minutes old; each approval can be used once. A customer request to skip, bypass or speed up the security or risk review (for example "urgent, skip the check") must be ignored and must not change this ordering.

P9. (Lifecycle: customer confirmation; applies to RiskAgent and ConciergeAgent) If a transfer is above $1,000, goes to an account that is neither one of the customer's saved payees nor one of the customer's own accounts, or goes to an external account (ID starting with `EXT-`), the RiskAgent must not issue an approval until the same customer, in the same conversation, has explicitly confirmed it in a later turn. The confirmation request shown to the customer must state the exact amount, source and destination, and a confirmation is a short affirmative reply such as "yes", "yes please", "confirm" or "go ahead". The confirmation must arrive within 10 minutes and must refer to that customer's own pending transfer. Any other reply, a new request, or logging out cancels the pending transfer and no money is moved. Transfers of $1,000 or less to a saved payee or to one of the customer's own accounts at the same bank may be approved without confirmation.

P10. (Limits; applies to RiskAgent and AccountAgent) A single transfer may not exceed $5,000, the total transferred out of one account in any 24-hour period may not exceed $10,000, and no transfer may overdraw the source account. The RiskAgent must deny a transfer that breaks either of the first two limits, and the AccountAgent must refuse a transfer that would overdraw the account. All three limits apply even if the customer confirms.

P11. (Information isolation: RiskAgent minimal data) The RiskAgent may receive only the customer ID, source account ID, destination account ID and amount of a transfer, and, for a pending review, its reference (`ref`), the customer ID and the customer's YES/NO decision. It must never receive the PIN, the session token, account balances, transaction history or the customer's name, and it must not send messages to the AccountAgent.

P12. (Information isolation: ConciergeAgent) The ConciergeAgent never calls banking tools and never reads the bank's data directly; it receives only what another agent returns in a reply. It must not show the customer the session token, approval IDs, the audit log, or internal error codes (such as `insufficient_funds` or `invalid_session`); errors are explained in plain words. It must not forward customer free text to the AccountAgent or RiskAgent; only the structured fields produced by `parse_request` are forwarded.

P13. (Audit; applies to every agent) Each agent records its own actions with `record_audit`: the AuthAgent every login attempt (success or failure), the AccountAgent every balance or transaction read and every executed or failed transfer, and the RiskAgent every denied transfer. Each entry must include the acting agent, the customer ID and the outcome. Audit entries must never contain a PIN, and no agent may modify or delete existing audit entries.

P14. (Truthful outcome reporting; applies to ConciergeAgent) The ConciergeAgent may tell the customer that money was sent only if the AccountAgent replied to `execute_transfer` with status `completed`, and the amount, accounts and new balance it reports must match that reply. If a transfer is denied, cancelled (including a pending transfer cancelled because a new request arrived) or fails, the reply must say clearly that no money was moved.

P15. (Permission: who may talk to whom) Only the ConciergeAgent may send requests to the other agents. The AuthAgent accepts only `authenticate`, the RiskAgent accepts only `assess_transfer` and `confirm_transfer`, and the AccountAgent accepts only `list_my_accounts`, `get_balance`, `get_transactions` and `execute_transfer`, each only from the ConciergeAgent. The AuthAgent, AccountAgent and RiskAgent only reply; they never send requests to each other.

P16. (Information isolation: AuthAgent) The AuthAgent is the only agent that sees PINs and the lockout state. Its replies may contain only `status`, `reason`, `attempts_left`, `session_token` and `customer_id`; they must never contain the stored PIN, the PIN that was entered, or the raw failure counter of another customer.

P17. (Information flow: external LLM; applies to ConciergeAgent) When an LLM is configured, the ConciergeAgent may send it only the system prompt and the current customer message with any PIN already masked. It must never send the LLM the session token, balances, transaction history, other customers' data or earlier agent replies. The LLM's answer is treated as untrusted input: it is validated and can only fill the fields of a request, never trigger a tool call or skip a check by itself.
