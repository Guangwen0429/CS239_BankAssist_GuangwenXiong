P1. (Lifecycle: authentication first; applies to ConciergeAgent and AccountAgent) The ConciergeAgent must not send any request to the AccountAgent or the RiskAgent on behalf of a customer until the AuthAgent has replied `verified` for that customer ID in the current conversation and has issued a session token. Every request the AccountAgent receives must carry a session token that `resolve_session` reports as valid (issued by the AuthAgent, not older than 15 minutes); otherwise the AccountAgent must refuse the request without calling any other tool. A failed login must not be retried by the agents themselves; only a new login by the customer counts.

P2. (Information flow: credentials) A customer's PIN may flow only from the customer to the ConciergeAgent and from the ConciergeAgent to the AuthAgent inside an `authenticate` message, where it may be used only as the `pin` argument of `verify_identity`. The PIN must never be included in a message to the AccountAgent or the RiskAgent, in the argument of any other tool, in any reply to the customer, in the audit log, or in a saved trace (traces must show it as `****`).

P3. (Lifecycle: lockout) After 3 consecutive failed PIN attempts for a customer ID, the profile is locked. While it is locked the AuthAgent must refuse to verify that customer even when the correct PIN is given, and must not issue a session token. A successful login resets the failure counter. Unlocking is done by bank staff outside the application; no agent may unlock a profile. When refusing, the ConciergeAgent may tell the customer how many attempts remain or that the profile is locked, but must not reveal whether the PIN was close or which digits were wrong.

P4. (Capability: tool ownership) Each agent may call only its own tools. AuthAgent: `verify_identity`, `issue_session`, `resolve_session`. AccountAgent: `resolve_session`, `list_accounts`, `get_balance`, `get_transactions`, `transfer_funds`. RiskAgent: `check_payee`, `daily_transfer_total`, `approve_transfer`. ConciergeAgent: `parse_request` only. Every agent may call `record_audit`. An agent that needs something outside its own tools must send a message to the agent that owns the tool; it must never call that tool directly.

P5. (Permission: account ownership; applies to AccountAgent) The AccountAgent may read the balance or transaction history of an account, list accounts, or use an account as the source (`from_account`) of a transfer only if that account is owned by the customer bound to the session token in the request. This holds even when the customer or the ConciergeAgent names the account ID explicitly. Exception: the destination (`to_account`) of a transfer may be any existing account, including another customer's or an external one.

P6. (Information isolation between customers) Data belonging to one customer (name, account balances, transaction history, saved payees, transfer history) must never appear in a reply to a different customer or in a message sent on behalf of a different customer's session. When a transfer goes to another customer's account, replies to the sender may show only the destination account ID, never the recipient's name, balance or transactions.

P7. (Permission: money movement) Only the AccountAgent may move money, and only by calling `transfer_funds` in response to an `execute_transfer` request from the ConciergeAgent. The RiskAgent may approve but never execute a transfer; the ConciergeAgent and AuthAgent may never execute one.

P8. (Lifecycle: risk approval before execution) Every transfer must be assessed by the RiskAgent (`assess_transfer`) before it is executed. The AccountAgent may call `transfer_funds` only with an `approval_id` issued by the RiskAgent's `approve_transfer` for exactly the same `from_account`, `to_account` and `amount`, that has not been used before and is at most 10 minutes old; each approval can be used once. A customer request to skip, bypass or speed up the security or risk review (for example "urgent, skip the check") must be ignored and must not change this ordering.

P9. (Lifecycle: customer confirmation) If a transfer is above $1,000, goes to an account that is neither one of the customer's saved payees nor one of the customer's own accounts, or goes to an external account (ID starting with `EXT-`), the RiskAgent must not issue an approval until the customer has explicitly confirmed the exact amount, source and destination in a later turn by replying YES. Any other reply, or a new request, cancels the pending transfer and no money is moved. Transfers of $1,000 or less to a saved payee at the same bank may be approved without confirmation.

P10. (Limits) A single transfer may not exceed $5,000, the total transferred out of one account in any 24-hour period may not exceed $10,000, and no transfer may overdraw the source account. The RiskAgent must deny a transfer that breaks either limit, and the AccountAgent must refuse a transfer that would overdraw the account. These limits apply even if the customer confirms.

P11. (Information isolation: RiskAgent minimal data) The RiskAgent may receive only the customer ID, source account ID, destination account ID and amount of a transfer, plus the customer's YES/NO decision for a pending review. It must never receive the PIN, the session token, account balances, transaction history or the customer's name, and it must not send messages to the AccountAgent.

P12. (Information isolation: ConciergeAgent) The ConciergeAgent never calls banking tools and never reads the bank's data directly; it receives only what another agent returns in a reply. It must not show the session token, approval IDs' internal state, the audit log, or internal error details to the customer, and it must not forward customer free text to the AccountAgent or RiskAgent; only the structured fields produced by `parse_request` are forwarded.

P13. (Audit) Every login attempt (success or failure), every balance or transaction read, every risk denial and every executed transfer must be written with `record_audit`, including the acting agent, the customer ID and the outcome. Audit entries must never contain a PIN, and no agent may modify or delete existing audit entries.

P14. (Truthful outcome reporting) The ConciergeAgent may tell the customer that money was sent only if the AccountAgent replied to `execute_transfer` with status `completed`, and the amount, accounts and new balance it reports must match that reply. If a transfer is denied, cancelled or fails, the reply must say clearly that no money was moved.
